# Content and artwork sources

## Park Scouts materials

The badge logo and park photograph come from the supplied 2025 Park Scouts report materials. The palm silhouette and crocodile head are transparent cutouts from that badge.

The collage images, group portrait, committee artwork, recognition star, and ten principle icons are raster captures extracted from the 2026 Scouts yearbook supplied for this project. These captures are not editable Canva elements or SVG originals. The Admin image is yearbook humor, rather than a portrait of a committee member.

2026 yearbook: https://www.canva.com/design/DAHBlztmx58/x0vCv4xD2ejrt0vrQGLd7A/edit

2026 report: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1SMadB0LnqBaidksFiFhaNOdu19cqafitHBq2dtViF9I/edit
